Research projects

I work on project evaluations at the JICA Ogata Sadako Research Institute for Peace and Development.

Sri Lanka

Walawe Left Bank Upgrading and Extension Project

I managed field data collection covering about 900 households, 300 agricultural markets, 200 farmer organizations, and more than 100 irrigation canals. I also organized public goods, ultimatum, and trust games as part of the project evaluation.

Uganda

Northern Uganda Farmers’ Livelihood Improvement Project

Working remotely, I analyzed baseline data and helped design the random assignment for the evaluation. I also supported the analysis of farm income, production, and other indicators of household livelihoods.
